Akshat's Research Project

     Hello and welcome to Akshat Tewari's website about his research project! Research was a fun and interesting experience. At High Technology High School, Research Practicum is a core component of one's time here. It teaches students about the scientific process and how to overcome certain obstacles to work toward their goal. In addition, it allows you to learn more about a field of science that may interest you. All in all, it's a journey that teaches people how to think like a scientist. This website documents Akshat's progress and his results throughout his research journey.

Background

In a world that was shaken by a viral pandemic, humankind shouldn't expose itself to another epidemic, one caused by microorganisms.
  • This experiment used direct current to shock yeast (Schizosaccharomyces Pombe)
  • This research project explored a novel way to treat fungal infection
  • Methods to treat fungal infections are aren't too well developed
  • While the results were not signifcant, this research project gives way to other researchers who plan to explore ways to treat fungal diseases

Rationale

This research aimed to address a gaping hole in the field of medical study
  • Experiments like these have yielded positive results in terms of inhibiting fungal growth
  • Despite this, research in this particular field of study is largely undeveloped.
  • This experiment aimed to expand a novel way to stop fungal infection
